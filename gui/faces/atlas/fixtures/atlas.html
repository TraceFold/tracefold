why-- which screen to open first

this was the last of the six screens built, which is a fact about the order they were made in and not about which one matters most. By design this is the screen to open first: a map of what has been touched, before choosing one of the other five questions.

internal reference -- names inside this codebase

Architecturally this face is meant to sit first, as the map a reader opens before choosing one of the other five questions (app req/08 SS2-2, Owner ruling 263); that claim is the separate `default_slot` declaration below, deliberately not folded into this build-order number so the two questions ("built which turn" and "sits where by design") stay answerable independently.
